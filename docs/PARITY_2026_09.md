Reference: Three.js `e334b8daf377056966523aff23b21ab30b475d36`.

## Delivered

- Identical balance, map and visual-style data: seven maps, updated infantry/vehicle costs and counters, per-victim splash damage.
- One to three AI enemies, free-for-all or coalition, four AI speeds. Coalition members share scouting and coordinate raids, offensives and assistance. Destroying every headquarters eliminates a faction; victory requires eliminating all rivals.
- Seven campaign stages with sequential unlocking, saved completion/best times and reinforcements for coalition stages. Saves remain local to the browser/device; they are not synchronized between editions.
- Paid Harvester building repair (10 HP/sec each, maximum two active workers). Engineers retain their free support role.
- Resource rally points automatically assign newly trained Harvesters to gathering; selected production buildings display rally markers.
- Sentinel combat-unit priority, stronger tower upgrades and additional range; AI tower construction and recovery of abandoned sites.
- Blocked-shot repositioning, finite retry behavior and movement progress checks. Explicit movement still interrupts combat for retreat/micro control.

## Engine-specific boundaries

Godot retains its native navigation, renderer, Blender models and compact touch controls. This update ports gameplay rules and shared data; it does not replace Godot rendering with Three.js shaders or port JavaScript DOM widgets. Multiplayer remains planned, not implemented. Automated mobile viewport checks do not establish physical Android frame rates.
